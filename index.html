The Woman Who Made Everything Possible

BY IMCHA & YANGER  ·  WITH LOVE  ·  NOVEMBER 10, 2026

On this Friday morning — just three days after Richard Nixon's historic landslide re-election — the world quietly received a far more significant event: the arrival of Ayao, the woman who would one day become our Mom. While the nation's newspapers were still ink-stained with election analysis, those in the know understood that the real headline belonged to the maternity ward.

Nurses on the floor reported an inexplicable warmth in the room. Doctors, who had seen many births, admitted this one felt different. And the radio in the waiting room was playing Johnny Nash's "I Can See Clearly Now" — which, we can only describe as the universe's most perfectly chosen soundtrack. It was a song about brightness, clarity, and sunshine — a song about Mom before Mom even had a name.

Born into an era of wide collars, shag carpets, and the Apollo programme's final act, she arrived at a world poised between the old and the dazzlingly new. She would go on to do what all great mothers do — build an entire world for someone else, and make it feel effortless.

"Before I had words for it, she was home. Happy Birthday, Mom."

💌 Letters to the Editor — Personal Dispatches

A Letter From Imcha and Yanger · Published on the Front Page, Where You Belong

Mom, they say the greatest things in life are impossible to put into words. We think that's because those things are usually you.

Fifty-four years ago today, on a Friday morning in November, while the world was busy with its own noise — elections, headlines, the last moonwalks — the most important thing that ever happened to our family, happened quietly. You arrived. And everything that came after, everything that matters, can be traced back to that moment.

You were born the same year as The Godfather, Pong, and M*A*S*H — things the world would call iconic. But in our house, in our story, the most iconic thing that came from 1972 is you. The woman who made home feel like home. Who showed up, every single time. Who gave more than anyone ever asked for, and made it look like the most natural thing in the world.

The #1 song the day you were born says it all: "I can see clearly now, the rain is gone… it's gonna be a bright, bright, sun-shiny day." That's what having you as a Mom has always felt like.

Happy Birthday, Ayao. Happy Birthday, Mom. ❤

— Imcha and Yanger, with more love than this newspaper can hold
